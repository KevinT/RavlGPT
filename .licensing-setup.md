## 💰 Dual-Licensing Strategy (Future)

If you want to offer commercial licenses later:

### Option A: Simple Commercial License
- Open source: MPL 2.0 (free, with copyleft)
- Commercial: Custom license (paid, proprietary use allowed)

### Option B: Support License
- Open source: MPL 2.0 (free, community support)
- Commercial: Same MPL 2.0 + SLA, support, warranties

### When to Consider Commercial Licensing:
- Companies want proprietary modifications
- Enterprise customers need SLA/support
- Cloud providers want to resell RAVL as a service

## 📞 Enforcement

### If Someone Violates MPL 2.0:

1. **Contact them politely** - Often it's accidental
2. **Request compliance** - Ask them to:
   - Include LICENSE file
   - Maintain copyright notices
   - Share modifications (if applicable)
3. **Document violation** - Keep records
4. **Consult lawyer** - If they refuse to comply

Most violations are unintentional and resolved with a friendly email.

## ❓ Common Questions

### Q: Can someone use RAVL in their commercial product?
**A:** Yes! They just need to keep your copyright notices and share modifications to RAVL files.

### Q: Can someone offer RAVL as a paid service?
**A:** Yes, under MPL 2.0. If you want to prevent this, you'd need AGPL or Commons Clause.

### Q: Can I change the license later?
**A:** You can for future versions, but past versions stay under MPL 2.0. Changing licenses requires agreement from all contributors.

### Q: Do I need to add copyright headers to every file?
**A:** Not required, but recommended for important files. The LICENSE file covers everything.

### Q: What if I want stronger protection?
**A:** You can switch to AGPL v3 (stronger copyleft) but this may reduce adoption.
